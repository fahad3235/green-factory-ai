# Architecture & Data Assumptions Specification
## Green Factory Brain AI (v2.0) — Rupsha Garments Ltd. (Fictional Demo)

### 1. System Architecture & Single Source of Truth
- **Centralized Store (`AppState`)**: All 12 primary views, the AI Copilot (English & বাংলা), the 6-agent AI Agent Console, and the 3D WebGL EPMS Digital Twin subscribe to a single reactive state tree initialized from a deterministic Mulberry32 PRNG (`seed = 20261018`).
- **Strict Data Classification**: Every metric displays an explicit badge:
  - `SIMULATION` — Synthetic telemetry generated by the deterministic physics/load engine.
  - `ESTIMATE/FORECAST` — Rule-based scenario calculations derived from current tariff and emission factors.
  - `SAMPLE/IMPORTED` — Historical baseline reference records.
  - `UNAVAILABLE` — Live PLC/SCADA write connectors or external LLM APIs when unconfigured.

### 2. Fleet Composition & Reconciliation (18 Monitored Units + EPMS Switchgear)
- **Sewing Department (8 units)**: `MCH-SEW-01` through `MCH-SEW-08` (Servo & clutch lockstitch/overlock lines).
- **Cutting Department (3 units)**: `MCH-CUT-01` through `MCH-CUT-03` (CNC auto-spreader & laser/knife cutters).
- **Finishing Department (4 units)**: `MCH-FIN-01` through `MCH-FIN-04` (Steam pressing, curing oven, thread suction).
- **Utilities & Auxiliaries (3 units)**: `COMP-01` (Rotary Screw Air Compressor, 55 kW rated), `BOIL-01` (Natural Gas Steam Boiler + Feed Pump), `LGT-ZN-01` & `LGT-ZN-02` (Production Hall & Warehouse Lighting Zones — 18 total fleet units).
- **EPMS Switchgear & Breakers**: `BREAKER_CBX-305` (Main Low-Voltage ACB Feeder), `TR-MAIN-01` (2,500 kVA Step-Down Transformer), `CAP-BANK-01` (Auto Power Factor Correction Bank, PF = 0.92).

### 3. Domain Calculation Formulas
- **Energy vs. Power**: Instantaneous active demand is tracked in `kW`; apparent demand is `kVA = kW / PF`; integrated consumption over time $\Delta t$ hours is `kWh = kW × Δt`.
- **Electricity Cost (BDT)**: `Cost (BDT) = Energy (kWh) × Tariff (BDT/kWh)`. Default blended industrial tariff: `৳ 12.40 / kWh` (`BPDB MT-3 Industrial Schedule v2026.1`; Peak `৳ 14.88`, Off-peak `৳ 11.16`).
- **Carbon Accounting (GHG Protocol Corporate Standard)**:
  - **Scope 1 (Direct Stationary Combustion)**: Natural gas consumed by `BOIL-01` (`m³ × 2.020 kg CO₂e/m³ ÷ 1000 = tCO₂e`). Baseline monthly Scope 1 = `4.91 tCO₂e`.
  - **Scope 2 (Purchased Grid Electricity)**: Grid electricity (`kWh × 0.560 kg CO₂e/kWh ÷ 1000 = tCO₂e`, DoE Bangladesh Grid Emission Factor v2025.2). For `52,300 kWh`, Scope 2 = `29.288 tCO₂e`.
  - **Total Monthly Emissions**: `4.91 + 29.288 = 34.198 tCO₂e` (displayed as `34.2 tCO₂e`). Purchased electricity is never misclassified as Scope 1.
- **Sustainability Score (0–100)**: Composite index (`35%` Energy Intensity vs target, `30%` Carbon Reduction vs baseline, `20%` Fleet Availability, `15%` Verified Savings ratio). Explicitly labeled `SIMULATION — NOT AUDITED OR CERTIFIED`.
